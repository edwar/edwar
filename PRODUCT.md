## Platform

web

## Users
Prospective clients and employers evaluating Edwar Amaya as a frontend developer: a mix of international companies and teams (remote, English) and local businesses or agencies in Colombia, Ecuador and LatAm (Spanish). They arrive to judge, in a few minutes, whether Edwar is good enough to hire, and the portfolio's own UI/UX is the first piece of evidence they see.

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio at www.edwaramaya.com that convinces visitors to contact Edwar for frontend work. Success is a visitor reaching out (WhatsApp or email) or opening the CV after seeing the work. The site must itself demonstrate the quality of UI/UX craft being sold.

## Positioning
A frontend developer who ships complete products end to end (UI, backend, database, deployment) rather than only interfaces, with visible care for UI/UX, motion, performance and accessibility. The portfolio is live proof of that care.

## Operating Context
Bilingual site (English at `/`, Spanish at `/es`), light and dark themes, a printable CV page (`/cv`, `/es/cv`). Astro 7 + Tailwind CSS 4 + GSAP, deployed on Vercel. Primary contact channels are WhatsApp and email (edwaramayadiaz@gmail.com); GitHub and LinkedIn are secondary.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- 12 published projects with live links: Saludtools, Pichincha Miles, Club Miles Ecuador (client work) and VLink, Hermes, Loki, Apolo, Perseus, Thor, Atenea, Nabu, CogniLab (own products, several under cognilab.dev).
- Skills shown: React, Next.js, Astro, Angular, Tailwind, JavaScript, TypeScript, HTML, CSS; PostgreSQL, Prisma, Firebase, Supabase; Git, GitHub, Jira, Bitbucket, Atlassian, Vercel.
- Both languages must stay in parity; copy lives in `src/i18n`.
- Must respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and keep strong SEO (sitemap, hreflang, JSON-LD already in place).

## Brand Commitments
Name: Edwar Amaya. Title currently "Frontend Developer" / "Desarrollador Frontend". Existing personal logo mark (`src/assets/cv.svg`) and photo (`src/assets/edwar.webp`).

Standing visual preference (chosen 2026-10-29 when offered a bolder world): play the category standard straight. Edwar wants the portfolio to sit alongside the best developer portfolios (Josh Comeau, Brittany Chiang tier): personal, very legible, restrained motion, work forward. Execute conventions at full craft, without irony or smuggled quirk. The previous look (dark ground, fuchsia-pink gradient, bento cards) is replaced, not polished.

## Evidence on Hand
Only the published projects and their live links, plus the CV content. No testimonials, client logos or impact metrics are available; future work must not fabricate any.

## Product Principles
1. The portfolio is the proof: every detail of its UI/UX should demonstrate the craft being sold.
2. Show real, shipped work first; claim nothing that cannot be verified.
3. Make contacting Edwar effortless from anywhere on the page, in either language.
4. Legibility and accessibility are part of the pitch, not a compliance afterthought.
5. Motion serves comprehension and delight, never blocks reading or contact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Accessibility is a stated differentiator: aim for WCAG AA contrast and keyboard/screen-reader support in both themes and both languages, and honor reduced-motion preferences.
